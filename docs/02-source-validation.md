## Investigation worksheet

1. Record observation date, owner, official source link, source terms, access restrictions and approved use. Stop on denied access; seek guidance or approved bulk access where unclear.
2. Capture exact new-notice, amendment/refile, release and withdrawal mappings and creditor interpretation. Exclude state and unrelated liens.
3. Establish authentication, viewing/download fees and lawful document access. Internal browser endpoints do not confer an API entitlement. No IP rotation or bypass after denial.
4. Exhaust pagination; detect caps and split windows where permitted. A still-capped day is incomplete, not a checkpoint success or zero result.
5. Inspect real permitted documents for name, approved contact fields, exact amount evidence, recorded date and stable instrument identity. Test text PDFs, scanned images, redactions and inconsistent totals.
6. Test a small authorized run from the intended host; record blocking, rate limits, bytes, runtime, cost and reviewer minutes. Source capability is not proven by a developer laptop alone.
7. Compare direct and optional vendor access only with separately approved source use, vendor cost and limits. No paid document acquisition by default.
8. Sign off a versioned mapping with allowed hosts, access basis, evidence references, types, time semantics, limits and county decision.

Keep real records and session details restricted. Only sanitized technical findings and aggregate counts belong in GitHub.

## Supply measurement

Target four complete historical recorded-date weeks for accessible counties without blocking the first end-to-end demonstration. Record actual covered dates and gaps. Count raw hits, true notices, distinct notice identities, cross-county duplicates, unique taxpayers, holds, exclusions, qualified prospects and client-accepted rows. Never add county totals before cross-county reconciliation.

Historical backfill and newly delivered weekly supply are separate cohorts. A few samples or two deliveries cannot guarantee future 50/week. Missing representative edge cases are coverage gaps, not permission to invent real-source fixtures.

## Three-week timing

- Days 1–2: investigate all four sources in parallel with foundation work; surface blockers immediately.
- Days 3–5: build the first complete path using the earliest demonstrably usable permitted source, not an assumed fixed county order.
- Days 6–10: extend approved coverage and measure actual manual effort and yield.
- Day 12: freeze adapter expansion; prioritize accuracy, repeated operation and handoff.

## County decision

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Pending | Evidence not reviewed | Disabled |
| Green | Permitted usable discovery and required evidence within approved limits | Eligible for implementation/testing, not automatic launch |
| Amber | Partial access or required evidence/capacity missing | No claim of qualified coverage; resolve or obtain accepted alternative |
| Red | Permission, access or cost blocker | Disabled; escalate |

All four counties remain in scope. Explicitly accepted manual assistance can provide coverage only with lawful evidence, provenance and capacity. Unavailable counties require a written amendment or pause. One working adapter is a milestone, not contractual completion.
