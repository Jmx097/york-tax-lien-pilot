# Decisions and launch gates

## Status and authority

**All gates below are pending.** Neither publication nor approval of this repository is agreement execution or operational/professional approval. Record approval identity, date, scope, evidence reference and review/expiry in the restricted client workspace. Public issues contain only sanitized progress and no private advice, credentials or client records.

Agreement finalization is paused while validation runs. The validation recommendation (GO TO AGREEMENT, NARROW SCOPE, NO-GO or NEEDS OWNER INPUT) comes before G01. Internal sanctioned discovery, collection, personal-data transfer, paid access and billing are distinct activities with distinct approvals. Do not proceed with an activity while its applicable gates remain unresolved. The commercial review must be completed before launching the contemplated referral-fee arrangement; no relabeling or disclosure-only assumption substitutes for review.

## Decision checkpoints

Source feasibility: no permitted exact-amount path in either state means NO-GO or NEEDS OWNER INPUT; one state means NARROW SCOPE. Offline MVP: core checks unreliable within the envelope means stop or narrow. QA and workload: persistent field errors or effort beyond the weekly budget block GO. Checkpoints are evidence gates, not calendar promises.

## Change control

Changing source or state scope, contact requirements, recency, spend, delivery channel or commercial terms requires authorized written acceptance and a versioned policy. Record effect on testing, staffing, costs and deadline. Do not silently replace missing phone/email with postal address, omit a state, activate Essex as verified IRS supply, or expand to New York, Texas or other geography.

Compensation and tax concerns are unresolved review questions, not findings of illegality or a tax ruling. Keep the actual agreement, rates, business tax identifiers, revenue records and advice in the restricted workspace; this public register records blockers only.
