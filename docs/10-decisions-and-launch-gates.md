# Decisions and launch gates

## Status and authority

**All gates below are pending.** Neither publication nor approval of this repository is agreement execution or operational/professional approval. Record approval identity, date, scope, evidence reference and review/expiry in the restricted client workspace. Public issues contain only sanitized progress and no private advice, credentials or client records.

Internal sanctioned discovery, collection, personal-data transfer, paid access and billing are distinct activities with distinct approvals. Do not proceed with an activity while its applicable gates remain unresolved. The commercial review must be completed before launching the contemplated referral-fee arrangement; no relabeling or disclosure-only assumption substitutes for review.

## Decision deadlines

Day 2 blocked source/fields: choose permitted accepted alternative or replan. Day 5 missing complete slice: stop expansion and escalate. Day 10 unstable core: cut optional work, not controls. Day 12 feature freeze. Dates are working-day targets after agreed start, not unconditional launch promises.

## Change control

Changing county scope, contact requirements, recency, spend, delivery channel or commercial terms requires authorized written acceptance and a versioned policy. Record effect on testing, staffing, costs and deadline. Do not silently replace missing phone/email with postal address, omit a county or expand to Cook/Harris/other geography.

Compensation and tax concerns are unresolved review questions, not findings of illegality or a tax ruling. Keep the actual agreement, rates, business tax identifiers, revenue records and advice in the restricted workspace; this public register records blockers only.
