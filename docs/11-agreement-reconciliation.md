## Open commercial decisions

Qualified professional-regulatory review of percentage/referral compensation must become a launch gate rather than an optional afterthought. This is a risk flag, not legal advice or an assertion that a specific arrangement is prohibited. Changing the label of a fee does not establish compliance.

Obtain a qualified determination of applicable invoice tax rather than hard-coding the draft's unconditional treatment. Complete attribution/collection windows and clarify pilot duration, eligible categories, exclusions, credits, refund/chargeback adjustments and post-termination treatment. Keep approved values restricted.

The optional small-integration wording conflicts with no-API exclusions if interpreted broadly; clarify as separately scoped optional work, not an estimate for the whole build. No new purchases, sending infrastructure, campaigns, enriched contacts or ongoing support are authorized here.

## Acceptance and payments

Client acceptance of supporting contact details is a prerequisite to payment setup. CSV upload alone does not establish acceptance, fee entitlement or consent to contact taxpayers. Firm-owned activity and actual receipt evidence must reconcile to stable accepted lead IDs without double counting consultation credits. Hosted invoicing is sufficient; no custom billing application or automatic charge is included.
