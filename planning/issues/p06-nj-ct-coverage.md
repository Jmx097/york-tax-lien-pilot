# [P06] Measure NJ/CT coverage and eligible supply

## Assignment

Owner role: source engineer and reviewer. Stage: 3–4, permitted integration to QA. Dependencies: P02 decisions, the P04 slice and P05 common rules.

## Tasks

- [ ] Add the second state's source only with independent permission, evidence and mapped document types.
- [ ] Reduce raw counts to eligible unique taxpayers: amount checks, cross-filing dedupe, releases, recency, suppressions and prior delivery.
- [ ] Record actual covered intervals and source lag; keep historical backfill separate from weekly supply.
- [ ] Escalate inaccessible sources and review bottlenecks.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Each state has an evidence-backed coverage decision. One viable state means NARROW SCOPE, not full coverage.
- [ ] Actual eligible weekly yield, manual effort and costs are reported, including any shortfall against the conditional target.
- [ ] Backfill, duplicates, Essex records of unverified origin and stale or unsupported rows never pad the target.

## Done and exclusions

A second review and sanitized source conclusions are required. Do not expand beyond NJ/CT or promise fifty recurring taxpayers. Deadline pressure never removes safety checks or silently omits a state.
