# [P02] Validate permitted access and evidence across four counties

## Assignment

Owner role: source engineer. Target: days 1–2 for initial decisions, then refine through day 7. Dependency: applicable access and data authorization from P01. This runs alongside P03.

## Tasks

- [ ] Independently investigate Dallas, Collin, Denton, and Bexar terms, exact document types, pagination and result caps, source lag, authentication, and fees.
- [ ] Inspect actual permitted evidence for amount and approved contact fields. Test access from the intended host only when authorized.
- [ ] Record a pending, green, amber, or red decision, with source references, allowed method, limits, and manual effort.
- [ ] Benchmark accessible historical weeks with covered intervals and a deduplicated funnel. Do not invent counts.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Four dated county decisions and evidence references exist. Shared portal branding is not treated as identical mappings or free documents.
- [ ] Denied, paid, capped, or incomplete paths stop or escalate. They are not bypassed and are not reported as a successful zero.
- [ ] Manual alternatives require client acceptance and sustainable capacity. A missing county requires a written amendment or pause.

## Done and exclusions

Technical findings stay sanitized. Actual source documents, taxpayer information, cookies, and approvals stay restricted. Do not rotate addresses to bypass a denial or make unapproved purchases. A four-week historical benchmark is a research goal where records are accessible, not permission to delay the first-path demonstration.
