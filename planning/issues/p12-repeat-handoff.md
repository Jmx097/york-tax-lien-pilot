# [P12] Repeat delivery and hand off a sustainable pilot

## Assignment

Owner role: product owner, operator, and firm approver. Target: days 13–15. Dependencies: P08, P10, P11, and the applicable launch gates.

## Tasks

- [ ] The operator and backup execute the runbook without builder intervention.
- [ ] Repeat the reviewed delivery on the agreed legitimate cutoff and cadence. Do not invent an accelerated cycle.
- [ ] Confirm actual county coverage, human effort, budget, access roster, recovery, and the next review date.
- [ ] Record an explicit go, a signed amended scope, or a pause, with residual-risk owners and client acknowledgement.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A named operator and backup can run, review, deliver, reconcile, stop, and recover.
- [ ] At least one approved live delivery exists if the gates clear. A second delivery happens only if the agreed calendar permits; otherwise document the observation extension.
- [ ] Four-county coverage or a signed change, real counts and costs, and client feedback are visible. There is no future-volume or revenue guarantee.

## Done and exclusions

Approvals and evidence remain restricted. GitHub receives sanitized progress only. Closing an issue does not create a live label, an ongoing support obligation, new geography, spending authority, or payment authority.
