# [P09] Set up the acceptance and collections reconciliation log

## Assignment

Owner role: product owner and firm coordinator. Target: days 6–11. Dependencies: approved attribution, commercial, and tax decisions from P01 before any live billing, plus stable identities from P07.

## Tasks

- [ ] Configure restricted acceptance, activity, and receipt logs with roles and a weekly update owner.
- [ ] Track unique actual receipts separately from bookings, engagement signatures, and unpaid invoices.
- [ ] Prevent consultation-credit double counting. Append linked refund and chargeback corrections.
- [ ] Reconcile approved fee and setup terms, any cap, and reversals in the restricted workspace. Compile the hosted invoice only after clearance.
- [ ] Resolve attribution and collection windows, disputes, tax treatment, and separate payment authorization before activation.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Client sample and contact-field acceptance are recorded before any payment setup.
- [ ] Receipt uniqueness and reversal checks pass without publishing financial evidence.
- [ ] No payable amount uses uncleared terms, unknown attribution, or an inferred tax rate.

## Done and exclusions

A named owner and second reviewer are required. Evidence stays restricted. Do not put the private agreement, rates, or client payments in GitHub. No custom payment application, card storage, automatic charge, guaranteed recovery, or legal or tax determination is included.
